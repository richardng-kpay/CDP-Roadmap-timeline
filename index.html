HARD GATE — Merchant OneID (Target: August 27, 2026)
What we cannot do properly without OneID:
  • Unified merchant profile — cannot resolve a single merchant across web, portal, app, and CRM. Same merchant appears as multiple disconnected records.
  • Multi-outlet identity — cannot link a merchant entity to its outlets (SID). One merchant with 3 outlets = 3 unrelated profiles. Segmentation and attribution break.
  • ICP scoring accuracy — ICP model inputs will be partial. Lookalike audiences built on incomplete profiles produce lower quality matches.
  • Attribution fidelity — campaign → MQL → SQL → activation chain cannot be reliably stitched without a canonical merchant ID as the spine.
  • Lifecycle trigger reliability — onboarding and re-engagement sequences may fire against incomplete or duplicate profiles.
What we CAN do before OneID is live:
  • Event taxonomy locked and schema enforced via Amplitude Govern
  • Compliance review completed (Rex + Benjamin + Jonas)
  • Data model designed with SID as canonical key — ready to activate once OneID delivers
  • Platform trial run — familiarisation with capabilities, sample data
  • Basic funnel analytics and session-level tracking live (user-level only, not merchant-level)
Escalation to Martin (COO): Confirm Aug 27 is a committed delivery date — not aspirational. If it slips, all Phase 1 use cases listed above are degraded until it lands. The programme can proceed in parallel, but the team and stakeholders must understand the trade-off explicitly.

Recommended framing to Martin: do not ask for confirmation of the full OneID build by Aug 27 — instead, identify the ~10% of OneID scope the Phase 1 pilot needs (merchant entity + outlet SID linkage for HK), and ask Martin to pressure Benjamin to prioritise that subset first. Smaller ask, harder to defer, more likely to unlock Phase 1 on schedule.
01The problem we're solving
Digital Growth cannot answer four basic questions today. Every acquisition and lifecycle decision depends on them.
Q1
Which merchants are worth acquiring?
No unified merchant identity across web, app, CRM, and payments. The same merchant appears as multiple disconnected records.
Cost: Acquisition decisions on intuition · ICP targeting impossible · ad spend wasted on merchants who never activate.
Q2
What does it cost to acquire them?
Attribution stops at form submission. KYB, activation, and first-transaction data are permanently invisible to Digital Growth.
Cost: CAC Payback cannot be measured · marketing optimises for lead volume, not merchant quality · Ayden launches with zero attribution.
Q3
Is lifecycle spend working?
HubSpot is email-only — no push, no in-app, no WhatsApp. Portal and app interactions invisible to marketing. Churn intervention is reactive.
Cost: Retention interventions arrive too late · multi-channel lifecycle structurally impossible · re-engagement is guesswork.
Q4
How fast can marketing actually move?
Every audience segment requires a Data team ticket. Turnaround >5 days, often 1–2 sprints. 40–75 hrs/week lost to manual reconciliation.
Cost: Slower to market than competitors · rapid-response campaigns impossible · execution speed capped by engineering availability.
02Recommended stack — CDP + CEP
Two tools that close the loop from campaign spend to GPV impact. CDP decides who to target. CEP decides when, on which channel, with what content.
Intelligence layer
Customer Data Platform (CDP)
  • Identity resolution — unified merchant ID across web, app, CRM, payments
  • AI audience segmentation — ICP-filtered cohorts, predictive churn, LTV scoring
  • Multi-touch attribution — campaign source through first transaction
  • Ask CDP — natural-language campaign analysis, no SQL required
  • Experiment — A/B testing on audiences and onboarding flows
  • Govern — schema enforcement, RBAC, PII masking, consent
  • Warehouse-native — bidirectional sync with Redshift
Activation layer
Customer Engagement Platform (CEP)
  • Push notifications — merchant app (iOS + Android)
  • In-app messaging — merchant portal behavioural triggers
  • Email — lifecycle journeys replacing HubSpot Marketing Hub
  • WhatsApp — HK CS pilot + merchant activation nudges
  • SMS — onboarding sequences and alerts
  • Sherpa AI — send-time, channel, content optimisation per merchant
  • Flows — visual journey builder, marketer self-serve
Native CDP → CEP connector. Real-time segment sync. Campaign results flow back into CDP for measurement. No custom middleware. Every campaign decision is measurable end-to-end.
